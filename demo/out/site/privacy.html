Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information The Cold Manual collects, why, and who can see it. The Cold Manual is ("we").

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, the access code you used, and your password in a scrambled (hashed) form that we can't read.
  • Payment details you send us: your payment screenshot, the transaction ID and the sender's name, number or account shown on it. We don't take card details.
  • Your work in the portal: lesson progress, quiz and roleplay results, drill scores, notes, your 30-day plan and everything you type into the tools.
  • Leads and clients you add: names, contact details and notes you put into the lead tracker and other tools.
  • Call requests: what you sell, your stage, what's holding you back, your WhatsApp number and best time, if you ask for a 1:1 call.
  • Questions in Ask the manual: questions without an answer are saved without your name, and we remove email addresses, phone numbers and links we can detect. Please don't type private client details.
  • Messages to us: whatever you send us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Technical details: your IP address is used for security limits, such as limiting login attempts.

How we use it

  • To confirm your payment, send your access code and run your account.
  • To save your work, so it's the same on your phone and laptop.
  • To give you support, handle refund requests and check progress for them.
  • To improve the training and write answers to questions members ask.
  • To keep the service secure, and to meet legal and record-keeping duties.

We don't sell your information.

Who can see it

  • Our team: people who run The Cold Manual, when they need to.
  • Your team manager: if you joined with a team code, your manager can see your progress, scores and 30-day plan status. You're told this before you join.
  • Companies that help us run the service: hosting, database and email providers. Their servers may be outside Pakistan.
  • Banks, JazzCash and WhatsApp: when you pay or message us through them, their own policies apply.
  • Authorities: if the law requires it.

Drills and speech

Your drill recordings stay in your browser, and we don't receive or store them. We keep your scores. The clarity score uses your browser's speech recognition. In Chrome and Edge, this may send your speech to the browser's maker to turn it into text, and their policies apply.

AI features

If AI features are switched on and you choose to use them, such as Ask AI, what you typed is sent to our AI provider to create the answer. These features are always optional. Answers to Ask AI questions may be saved, without your name, so we can check them and add them to our written answers.

Cookies and browser storage

We use one cookie to keep you logged in, and browser storage for settings such as your light or dark theme. We don't use advertising or analytics trackers on this site today. If we add them, for example to measure ads, we'll update this policy first. If you play a founder video, it loads from YouTube's privacy-enhanced service, and YouTube's policy applies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and work until you ask us to delete them or we close the service. We keep payment records for as long as we need them for accounting and legal reasons. Saved questions have no name attached, and we may keep them to improve the answers.

Your choices

  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it.
  • Download your data from Settings, or export your leads as a CSV from the lead tracker, at any time.
  • Reset your progress or change your password in Settings.

Deleting your account removes your work in the portal. We may still keep payment records as described above.

Keeping it safe

We use hashed passwords, secure login cookies, encrypted connections and limits on login attempts. No system is perfectly secure, so use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else.

Children

The Cold Manual is for people aged 18 and over, or younger people who have a parent or guardian's permission.

Changes

We'll change the date at the top when we update this policy, and announce important changes on the site or by email.
